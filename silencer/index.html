Silencer

电视去广告，换干净主屏

Silencer 帮你把安卓电视收拾干净：停用预装的广告和统计应用，换上干净的新主屏，手机还能当遥控器。只停用，不删除，随时能还原。

需要什么

  • 安卓系统的电视或盒子（Android TV 或 Google TV）。三星、LG 等不是安卓系统的电视用不了。
  • iPhone 和电视连在同一个 Wi-Fi 下。
  • 在电视的开发者选项里打开 ADB 调试（USB 调试、网络调试或无线调试）。App 里的「设置电视」按品牌一步一步教。

常见问题

  • 找不到电视：确认手机和电视在同一个 Wi-Fi 下、电视已打开 ADB 调试。第一次连接时，电视上会弹出是否允许调试，选「允许」（建议勾选「一律允许」）。
  • 小米电视：每次重启都会自己关掉 ADB 调试，以后要换主题或还原，先在电视上重新打开。
  • 换不了主屏：有些电视不让换主屏。Silencer 连上后会告诉你，这时可以只停用应用，或只把它当遥控器用。
  • 想恢复原样：在「我的电视」里点「还原」，电视会回到清理前的样子。

Silencer

No ads, a clean TV home

Silencer tidies up your Android TV: it switches off the preinstalled ad and tracking apps, puts on a clean new home screen, and turns your iPhone into the remote. Apps are only switched off, never deleted, and you can put everything back at any time.

What you need

  • A TV or box that runs Android (Android TV or Google TV). Samsung, LG and other TVs that don't run Android won't work.
  • Your iPhone and TV on the same Wi-Fi.
  • ADB debugging (USB debugging, Network debugging or Wireless debugging) switched on in the TV's developer options. Set up a TV in the app shows you how, step by step, for your TV's maker.

Common questions

  • It can't find my TV: check that your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi and that ADB debugging is on. The first time it connects, the TV asks whether to allow debugging: choose Allow (ticking Always allow helps).
  • Xiaomi TVs switch ADB debugging off at every restart. To change themes or restore later, switch it back on first.
  • The home screen can't be changed: some TVs don't allow it. Silencer tells you once it's connected; you can then just switch apps off, or use it only as a remote.
  • Putting it back: in My TV, tap Restore, and the TV goes back to how it was before the cleanup.
