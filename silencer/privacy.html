隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

数据处理

Silencer 不收集、上传、出售或共享个人数据。应用无需注册账号，没有广告、分析或第三方追踪服务，也不连接开发者的服务器。

设备本地数据

你添加的电视（名称和局域网地址）、清理前的备份和连接电视用的密钥只保存在你的 iPhone 上，密钥存放在系统钥匙串里。清理前的备份也会在电视上存一份，用于还原。这些信息不会发送给开发者。

网络与系统权限

Silencer 请求「本地网络」权限，只用来搜索和连接同一 Wi-Fi 下的电视。它不访问互联网。

你选择用照片做电视背景时，系统的照片选择器只把你选中的那一张交给 Silencer，Silencer 把它发送到你的电视上，不能读取你的相册。Silencer 不请求相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录或其他个人数据权限。

删除与保留

卸载应用会移除其本地数据。在应用里「还原」电视，会让电视回到清理前的样子。系统备份与设备迁移由 Apple 的系统设置管理。

联系我们

隐私相关问题请发送至 aceisgenius_cn@outlook.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Data Handling

Silencer does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data. The app requires no account, contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services, and does not connect to any server of the developer.

On-device Data

The TVs you add (their names and local network addresses), the backups taken before a cleanup, and the key used to connect to your TV are stored only on your iPhone; the key is kept in the system keychain. A copy of the backup is also kept on the TV so it can be restored. None of this is sent to the developer.

Network and System Permissions

Silencer asks for Local Network access only to find and connect to TVs on the same Wi-Fi. It does not access the internet.

When you choose a photo as your TV's background, the system photo picker gives Silencer only the photo you picked, which Silencer sends to your TV; it cannot read your photo library. Silencer does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or other personal data.

Deletion and Retention

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Restoring your TV in the app puts the TV back the way it was before the cleanup. System backups and device migration are governed by your Apple system settings.
