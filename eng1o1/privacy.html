ENG1O1 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

ENG1O1 是一款背英语单词的 App，由个人开发者开发（下面称「我们」）。这里说明 App 会用到哪些信息、怎么处理。未满 14 岁的用户，请和家长一起阅读，家长同意后再使用。

1. 不用注册，不收集身份信息：App 不需要注册或登录，不会收集你的姓名、手机号、位置、照片、通讯录等个人信息。

2. 学习记录只存在本机：学过的单词、练习和复习记录只保存在你的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器；删除 App 就会一并删除。

3. 「读一读」的录音：在「读一读」的卡片上，麦克风会保持打开，这样你开口的第一个音也能录全；声音只在手机内存里暂存约半秒，不写进存储。只有你按住录音按钮的这一段（连同按下前约半秒、松手后约 0.3 秒）会和要读的单词一起，通过加密连接发给腾讯云的口语评测服务打分；往上滑取消的录音不会发出。我们不在设备上保存录音，也没有自己的服务器接收录音。每次打分只带一个随机编号，不和你的身份关联。腾讯云对录音的处理遵守它自己的隐私政策和数据处理条款。

4. 麦克风权限：第一次用到「读一读」时，App 会请你允许使用麦克风。不允许也没关系：可以自己判断读对了没有，其他功能照常使用。你随时可以在 iPhone 的「设置」里关掉。

5. 什么时候联网：只有「读一读」打分时会联网。没有网络时，其他功能都能正常使用。

6. 无广告、无第三方统计：App 不含广告，不使用任何第三方统计或追踪工具。除了上面说的腾讯云打分，不会把你的信息提供给任何人。

7. 儿童隐私：未满 14 岁的孩子使用「读一读」前，需要家长同意。孩子的录音只按第 3 条用于打分，不做别的用途。家长不希望孩子的录音发出去的话，可以不给 App 麦克风权限。

8. 你的权利：学习记录在你自己的设备上，删除 App 就能全部删除；麦克风权限随时可以关闭。我们不保存你的录音和其他个人信息，所以没有需要我们代为删除的数据。

9. TestFlight 测试：如果你通过 TestFlight 提交反馈，Apple 会把你提交的截图和文字，以及崩溃报告、设备型号、系统版本等信息转给我们，我们只用于修复问题和改进 App。

10. 政策更新：政策有变化时，会更新这一页并写明更新日期；如果要新增收集或使用信息的方式，会先在 App 里告诉你。

11. 联系我们：如有问题，请发邮件至 aceisgenius_cn@outlook.com。

ENG1O1 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ENG1O1 is an app for learning English words, made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. If you are under 14, please read it with a parent, and use the app only with their consent.

1. No sign-up, no personal details: the app needs no sign-up or sign-in and collects no personal information, such as your name, phone number, location, photos or contacts.

2. Learning records stay on your device: the words learned and the practice and review records are stored only on your device and are never uploaded; deleting the app deletes them.

3. Recordings for reading aloud (读一读): while a reading card is on screen, the microphone stays on so that the first sound you say is caught; the sound is held only in the phone's memory, for about half a second, and never written to storage. Only what you record while holding the button (with about half a second before you press and 0.3 seconds after you let go) is sent, together with the word being read, over an encrypted connection to Tencent Cloud's speech assessment service to be scored; a recording cancelled by sliding up is never sent. We keep no recordings on the device and have no server of our own that receives them. Each scoring request carries only a random ID and is not linked to who you are. Tencent Cloud handles the recordings under its own privacy policy and data processing terms.

4. Microphone permission: the first time you reach a reading card, the app asks to use the microphone. You can say no: you then judge for yourself whether you read the word right, and everything else works as usual. You can turn it off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

5. When the app goes online: only to score reading aloud. Without a connection, everything else works.

6. No ads, no third-party analytics: the app shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking. Apart from the Tencent Cloud scoring above, your information is given to no one.

7. Children's privacy: a child under 14 needs a parent's consent before using reading aloud. A child's recordings are used only for scoring as described in point 3, and for nothing else. Parents who do not want a child's recordings sent can leave the app without microphone access.

8. Your rights: your learning records are on your own device, and deleting the app deletes them all; microphone access can be turned off at any time. We keep none of your recordings or other personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

9. TestFlight testing: if you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple shares the screenshots and comments you submit, along with crash reports and details such as device model and system version, with us. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

10. Changes: when this policy changes, this page is updated with a new date; if we ever plan a new way of collecting or using information, we will tell you in the app first.

11. Contact: for any questions, email aceisgenius_cn@outlook.com.
